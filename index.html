Founder · Assistive Robotics

Connecting robotics innovation with people who can benefit from greater independence.

FIRST AID Robotics was initiated by MegaKnytes FTC Team 11093 in partnership with FIRST. The program brings robotics teams together to design and build Simple Task Assist Robots (STARs) for people with mobility limitations.

Shared Designs

Build a repository of free STAR designs that robotics teams can use, improve, and share.

Families

Connect families with existing STAR designs or teams that can help develop more customized solutions.

FIRST Teams

Give robotics teams a way to apply design, fabrication, controls, and outreach skills to assistive engineering.

Sponsors & Mentors

Bring design expertise and resources together to support materials, development, and program growth.
